The Prelude

Why This Conclave?

The National Bamboo Conclave 2026 aims to bring together experts, policymakers, entrepreneurs, and stakeholders to collectively strengthen the bamboo ecosystem. It serves as a platform for knowledge exchange and innovation, highlighting bamboo as a climate-smart, renewable resource.

Key Objectives

  • Enhance understanding of diverse advantages and utilizations of bamboo in day-to-day life.
  • Highlight innovative technologies including engineered bamboo and value chain opportunities.
  • Discuss policy frameworks and government initiatives.

Why Bamboo?

1,600+ Species Worldwide
₹10 Lakh Crore Projected Market by 2030
Bamboo derived products valued between ₹5 - ₹6 Lakh Crore in 2024.

Bamboo captures carbon, preserves soil, and drives the green economy.

About Bharateeyam Trust

Bharateeyam, a charitable trust, came into existence in 2017 with the sole objective to serve the Indian subcontinent through different initiatives. It was founded by a group of intellectuals, comprising professionals, educators, and specialists from diverse fields. The trust's principal objective is to discuss, debate, and reflect on matters important to nation-building. Furthermore, the trust lays a great focus on the preservation of indigenous knowledge systems and their use for the betterment of society.

Enhancing these preliminary efforts, the Trust has committed itself further by organizing several conferences, seminars, and discussions pertaining to emerging national and international issues such as demonetization, bilateral ties, National Education Policy-2020, boundary disputes, water and environmental issues, and Jagannath culture.
